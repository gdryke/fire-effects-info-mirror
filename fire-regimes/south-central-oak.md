# Fire regimes of South-central oak communities

**Type:** Report
  
**Written:** March, 2026

## Citation

In: Fire Effects Information System, [Online]. U.S. Department of Agriculture, Forest Service, Rocky Mountain Research Station, Missoula Fire Sciences Laboratory (Producer). Available: https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis/fire-regimes/South-central-oak

## Introduction

A complete Fire Regime Synthesis for South-central oak communities has not yet been published in the Fire Effects Information System. However, LANDFIRE succession modeling of South-central oak Biophysical Settings (BpS) provides estimates of fire regime characteristics (see LANDFIRE Biophysical Settings section). Figure 1 shows where these BpSs occur.
