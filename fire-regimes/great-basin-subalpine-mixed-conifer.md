**Written:** February, 2026

## Introduction

A complete Fire Regime Synthesis for Great Basin subalpine mixed-conifer communities has not yet been published in the Fire Effects Information System. However, LANDFIRE succession modeling of Great Basin subalpine mixed-conifer Biophysical Settings (BpS) provides estimates of fire regime characteristics (see LANDFIRE Biophysical Settings section). Figure 1 shows where these BpSs occur.
