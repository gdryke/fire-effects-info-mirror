**Type:** Report
  
**Written:** April, 2026

## Introduction

A complete Fire Regime Synthesis for Appalachian mixed-hardwood communities has not yet been published in the Fire Effects Information System. However, LANDFIRE succession modeling of Appalachian mixed-hardwood Biophysical Settings (BpS) provides estimates of fire regime characteristics (see LANDFIRE Biophysical Settings section). Figure 1 shows where these BpSs occur.
