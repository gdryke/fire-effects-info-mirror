# Fire Effects Information System (FEIS) Mirror

This site is a mirror of the [USDA Fire Effects Information System (FEIS)](https://research.fs.usda.gov/feis), maintained for preservation and accessibility purposes. FEIS provides comprehensive literature reviews covering fire ecology of plants and animals, fire regimes of plant communities, and fire research studies.

All content is public domain (U.S. Government work).
